Enterprise Email Security Health Check

Review business email systems for secure mail flow, authentication, anti-phishing defenses, and operational readiness.

Karatin Government Solutions6 min read
Article overview ← Back to Resources Data Security 6 min read

Review business email systems for secure mail flow, authentication, anti-phishing defenses, and operational readiness.

Mail flow

Review sending and receiving domains, connectors, gateways, routing rules, and message tracing or diagnostic capabilities available in the selected email platform.

Authentication

Validate SPF, DKIM, DMARC, strong authentication, and exposure from outdated protocols or insecure relay paths.

Routing and policy rules

Identify outdated, duplicated, overly broad, or conflicting routing, forwarding, and mail security rules.

Mailbox protection

Assess anti-phishing, impersonation protection, URL filtering, attachment scanning, and additional safeguards for high-risk users.

Operations

Confirm monitoring, escalation, documentation, and ownership for incidents, changes, and recurring email problems.
