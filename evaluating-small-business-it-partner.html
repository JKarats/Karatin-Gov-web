Evaluating a Small-Business IT Partner

Assess expertise, delivery approach, communication, risk controls, and scalability.

Relevant expertise

Look for experience that directly matches the requirement.

Delivery approach

Review staffing, ownership, quality control, documentation, and escalation.

Communication

Confirm response expectations, reporting, stakeholder access, and issue ownership.

Risk controls

Evaluate security, privacy, continuity, and subcontractor management.

Scalability

Understand surge support, teaming relationships, and geographic coverage.
