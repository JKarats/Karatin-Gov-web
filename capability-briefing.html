Introduction

We help organizations protect sensitive data and adopt AI with confidence.

Karatin brings data security, compliance, AI governance, and practical training together in one focused engagement.

  • Data Security & Compliance: Discover, classify, label, protect, and govern sensitive information.
  • Responsible AI Adoption: Establish readiness, guardrails, accountability, and safe-use practices.
  • Training & Enablement: Build role-based knowledge that makes secure behavior repeatable.
02

Data Before AI

Secure the data foundation before expanding AI.

  • Sensitive information may be broadly accessible.
  • Sharing practices may exceed business need.
  • Unstructured data may lack classification or ownership.
  • Retention and compliance rules may be inconsistently applied.
  • Employees need clear guidance for responsible AI use.

Goal: Know what data exists, who can access it, and how it should be protected.

03

Data Security & Compliance

Protect sensitive information throughout its lifecycle.

  • Sensitive-data discovery and classification
  • Information protection, labeling, and handling rules
  • Data Loss Prevention policy design and improvement
  • Retention, records, and data-lifecycle management
  • Privacy, audit, eDiscovery, and compliance readiness
  • Risk assessments, control-gap analysis, and remediation guidance

Outcome: clearer data ownership, stronger controls, and practical evidence of readiness.

04

Responsible AI Adoption

Adopt AI with governance, guardrails, and confidence.

  • AI readiness and use-case assessments
  • Data exposure, oversharing, and permission-risk reviews
  • AI governance frameworks and acceptable-use policies
  • Responsible AI controls, roles, and decision accountability
  • Pilot planning, risk tracking, and adoption guidance
  • Ongoing monitoring and governance improvement

Outcome: productivity gains with protected data, clear accountability, and lower risk.

05

Training & Enablement

Training makes secure behavior repeatable.

  • Leaders: AI governance, risk ownership, oversight, and decision responsibilities.
  • Administrators: Data-security controls, policy operations, investigation, and reporting.
  • Employees: Sensitive-data handling, responsible AI use, privacy, and safe collaboration.
  • Teams: Role-based workshops, tabletop scenarios, readiness sessions, and job aids.
06

Data & AI Readiness Assessment

Turn assessment findings into sustainable action.

  • Discover: Confirm priorities, use cases, and data concerns.
  • Assess: Review exposure, sharing, controls, and governance.
  • Prioritize: Rank risks by mission impact, urgency, and effort.
  • Deliver: Provide findings, roadmap, and stakeholder briefing.
  • Design: Define controls, governance, learning objectives, and success measures.
  • Enable: Implement prioritized improvements and deliver role-based training.
  • Sustain: Measure adoption, review risk, and strengthen governance over time.

Deliverables: risk summary • prioritized recommendations • implementation roadmap • leadership briefing
