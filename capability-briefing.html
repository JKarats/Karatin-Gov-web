A flexible staffing engagement

A straightforward process tailored to each requirement.

  1. Request — Define roles, scope, location, qualifications, schedule, and contracting path.
  2. Align & Review — Discuss required skills, potential sourcing approach, screening, and candidate fit.
  3. Mobilize & Support — Confirm engagement terms, onboarding needs, communication, and ongoing support.

Possible engagement formats

Individual specialists • Project-based teams • Surge augmentation • Prime teaming support

Mobilization and candidate timelines depend on role requirements, screening, and availability; no fixed turnaround is implied.
