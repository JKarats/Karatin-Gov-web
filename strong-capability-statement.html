How to Build a Strong Capability Statement

Create a concise document that communicates relevance, proof, and contracting data.

Karatin Government Solutions6 min readUpdated August 2026
Article overview ← Back to Resources Government Contracting 6 min read Updated August 2026

Create a concise document that communicates relevance, proof, and contracting data.

Focus on the buyer

Tailor the document to the agency, opportunity, or prime.

Use clear competencies

Describe outcomes and capabilities instead of broad marketing claims.

Show differentiators

Explain why the company is particularly suited to the requirement.

Include proof

Use relevant experience, methods, metrics, or partner capacity.

Make data easy to find

Display UEI, CAGE, NAICS, certifications, contacts, and website clearly.
