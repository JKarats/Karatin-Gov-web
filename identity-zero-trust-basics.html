Strengthen identity assurance, least privilege, device trust, and continuous verification.

Verify explicitly

Use identity, device, location, risk, application, and session context.

Use least privilege

Reduce standing access and separate administrative responsibilities.

Assume breach

Monitor risky activity and design controls to limit lateral movement.

Protect privileged accounts

Use strong authentication, dedicated admin accounts, and controlled workstations.

Review continuously

Remove stale roles, service accounts, exceptions, and unused applications.
