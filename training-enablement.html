What customers receive

More than a slide deck.

We organize training around practice, reinforcement, and knowledge transfer, with deliverables agreed as part of the engagement.

  • Targeted learning plan — audience, objectives, prerequisites, and scope.
  • Instructor-led sessions — demonstrations, exercises, and relevant use cases.
  • Reference materials — quick-start guides, checklists, job aids, or playbooks.
  • Knowledge checks — pre/post questions or task demonstrations where appropriate.
  • Reinforcement plan — follow-up coaching or check-ins when included in scope.

Learning effectiveness

KPIs that show whether training worked.

We can establish a baseline and agree measurable targets with each customer before delivery.

01

Attendance & completion

Track who participated and completed the agreed learning activities.

KPI: Completed ÷ Enrolled (%)
02

Knowledge improvement

Compare pre-training and post-training understanding or demonstrated skills.

KPI: Post-score − Pre-score
03

Training usefulness

Collect learner feedback about clarity, relevance, and confidence to apply concepts.

KPI: Learner feedback rating
04

On-the-job application

Review whether learners are using the new process or skill after the session.

KPI: Optional 30-day follow-up

Important: These are proposed reporting measures, not historical Karatin performance figures or guaranteed improvements. Targets, data collection, and follow-up timing are agreed during program scoping.

Common questions

Training FAQs

Do you tailor the training to our environment?

Yes. Scope, learning objectives, audience, approved technologies, and realistic scenarios can be tailored during intake.

Can you support both technical and nontechnical teams?

Training can be organized by role, including IT administrators, operational staff, leaders, and end users.

How do we measure whether it helped?

We can agree on baseline questions, post-session checks, attendance tracking, feedback surveys, and optional follow-up measures.

Do participants receive certifications?

Karatin can discuss completion records; third-party certifications are not implied unless specifically authorized and included in the engagement.
