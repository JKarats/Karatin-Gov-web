Common Questions

How Karatin helps organizations protect data and adopt AI responsibly.

Who does Karatin LLC serve?

Karatin supports federal agencies, state and local governments, educational institutions, prime contractors, and commercial organizations.

What does Karatin help organizations protect?

Karatin helps identify, classify, protect, and govern sensitive information while reducing inappropriate access, oversharing, data leakage, and compliance risk.

How does Karatin support secure AI adoption?

Karatin reviews data exposure, permissions, classification, governance, and responsible-use controls before AI expands access to organizational information.

Does Karatin provide staffing and training?

Yes. Karatin provides specialized professionals and practical role-based training alongside consulting, remediation, governance, and AI-readiness services.
