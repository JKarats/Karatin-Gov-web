Common Questions

How Karatin helps organizations secure data and prepare for AI.

What does Karatin LLC help organizations protect?

Karatin helps organizations identify, classify, protect, and govern sensitive information across Microsoft 365 while reducing inappropriate access, oversharing, data leakage, and compliance risk.

How does Karatin prepare an organization for AI adoption?

Karatin reviews data exposure, sharing, permissions, classification, governance, and responsible-use controls before AI expands access to organizational information.

Does Karatin work with government agencies and contractors?

Yes. Karatin supports government agencies, prime contractors, and teaming partners with focused data security, compliance, responsible AI, training, and readiness services.

What type of training does Karatin provide?

Karatin provides short, role-based training for leaders, administrators, employees, and teams so secure data handling and responsible AI practices become repeatable behaviors.
