Commitment to fair opportunity

Karatin LLC supports equal employment opportunity and intends to make recruitment, referral, and employment-related decisions based on relevant qualifications, role requirements, and legitimate business needs, consistent with applicable law.

Nondiscrimination

Karatin LLC does not support unlawful discrimination or discriminatory referral preferences based on race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy and related conditions), national origin, age, disability, genetic information, or any other status protected by applicable law. We will not knowingly honor discriminatory client requests in recruiting or candidate referrals.

Job-related requirements

Certain customer engagements may have lawful job-related eligibility requirements, such as security-clearance eligibility, citizenship requirements, or other conditions tied to a role or government contract. Such requirements are evaluated only when appropriate and supported by applicable law or contract terms. A candidate’s eligibility for a specific role does not imply an organization-wide hiring restriction.

Accommodation and accessibility

Individuals who need a reasonable accommodation to participate in an application or recruiting process may contact info@KaratinLLC.com or call (469) 269-0784. Requests are considered individually, consistent with applicable requirements.

Reporting a concern

To raise a concern about recruiting, referral, or employment-related treatment involving Karatin LLC, contact info@KaratinLLC.com. Individuals also retain rights to contact appropriate government agencies and exercise legally protected rights.

Questions or feedback?

Contact info@KaratinLLC.com or (469) 269-0784.
