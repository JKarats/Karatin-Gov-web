Assess technical fit, responsiveness, documentation, risk, and opportunity alignment.

Opportunity fit

Confirm scope, customer, contract vehicle, timeline, and role.

Technical fit

Map capabilities to specific work packages and proposal sections.

Business fit

Review certifications, capacity, insurance, accounting, and subcontract terms.

Working relationship

Define communication, ownership, deadlines, and data handling.

Post-award readiness

Prepare staffing, onboarding, reporting, quality control, and invoicing.
