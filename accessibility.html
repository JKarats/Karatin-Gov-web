Accessibility Statement

Our goal is to make Karatin’s information and services easier to use for everyone.

Our approach

Karatin LLC values access to information and aims to improve the usability of this website for people with different abilities and assistive technologies. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a reference when evaluating improvements; this statement does not claim that the entire website has been independently certified or audited for a particular conformance level.

Website features

We seek to maintain readable content, clear links and headings, keyboard-friendly interactions, responsive page layouts, and meaningful descriptions for important images. Some pages, linked documents, or third-party tools may not yet provide an equally accessible experience.

Need assistance?

If a page, form, or downloadable document is difficult to access, please tell us what you are trying to do. You may contact info@KaratinLLC.com or call (469) 269-0784. We will review requests and work with you to identify an appropriate way to access the information or complete the task.

What to include in feedback

When possible, send the web address or document name, a short description of the barrier, the device or browser you were using, and the format or assistance that would work for you. Please do not send confidential medical information to describe an accessibility issue.

Third-party content

External booking services, linked platforms, and embedded or downloadable third-party materials may have separate accessibility practices. Please report access issues to us when they affect your ability to use our site.

Questions or feedback?

Contact info@KaratinLLC.com or (469) 269-0784.
