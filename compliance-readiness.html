Common questions

Helpful answers before we begin

Does Karatin certify HIPAA, GDPR, FedRAMP, or CMMC compliance?

No. Karatin can provide practical readiness and documentation support; legal advice, formal assessments, and certifications remain with qualified or authorized parties.

Can we start with a narrow requirement?

Yes. Engagements may focus on a specific policy, process, document set, data type, or readiness topic.

Can you help with retention and eDiscovery preparation?

Yes. Examples include retention requirements, records governance, role responsibilities, preservation workflows, and documentation.

Is training included?

Training and enablement can be scoped for relevant stakeholders and business users.
