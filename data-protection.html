Common questions

Helpful answers before we begin

Does Karatin focus only on Microsoft 365?

Microsoft 365 is a core area of expertise. Other data protection needs can be assessed against the available scope and technical requirements.

Is this the same as penetration testing?

No. This service focuses on information governance, access, classification, handling controls, and practical protection workflows.

Can data protection work support AI readiness?

Yes. Better data ownership, access and sensitivity controls can reduce exposure before AI systems make information easier to discover.

What is typically delivered?

The deliverables depend on scope and may include findings, configuration recommendations, a remediation backlog, and staff enablement materials.
