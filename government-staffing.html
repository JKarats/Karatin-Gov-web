Frequently asked questions

Do you already have cleared staff?

Do not assume cleared personnel are immediately available. Clearance levels, active status, citizenship, and screening must be confirmed for each proposed candidate and assignment.

Can we buy through a prime contractor?

Teaming or subcontracting under a prime contractor may be an option, subject to the prime’s process and the contract requirements.

What buying paths are available?

Potential routes include eligible direct procurement and subcontracting through an existing prime or authorized contract channel. Karatin should not be assumed to hold a particular contract vehicle.
