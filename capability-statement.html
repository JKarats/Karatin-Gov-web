Capability Narrative

Right-sized consulting, staffing, and training support.

Karatin LLC provides specialized consulting and staffing services that help federal agencies, prime contractors, and regulated organizations protect sensitive data, meet compliance obligations, prepare for responsible AI adoption, and strengthen workforce readiness. We deliver defined assessments, remediation, governance, and training projects — or provide experienced professionals to support customer teams for short-term, surge, and long-term needs.
