Review mail flow, authentication, transport rules, security, and operational readiness.

Mail flow

Review connectors, accepted domains, centralized transport, third-party gateways, and message trace patterns.

Authentication

Validate SPF, DKIM, DMARC, modern authentication, and legacy protocol exposure.

Transport rules

Identify outdated, duplicated, overly broad, or conflicting mail flow rules.

Mailbox protection

Review anti-phishing, impersonation protection, Safe Links, Safe Attachments, and high-risk users.

Operations

Confirm monitoring, escalation, documentation, and ownership for incidents and changes.
