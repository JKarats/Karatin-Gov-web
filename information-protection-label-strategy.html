Build a sensitivity-label structure users can understand and consistently apply.

Keep labels understandable

A small, clear hierarchy usually works better than a complex taxonomy.

Define protection behavior

Document encryption, access, marking, sharing, and downgrade rules.

Use automation selectively

Apply recommended or automatic labeling where confidence is high.

Test collaboration

Validate internal sharing, external recipients, mobile use, and application compatibility.

Measure adoption

Review labeling patterns, user feedback, overrides, and policy incidents.
