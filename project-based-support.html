Frequently asked questions

Can project capacity change during delivery?

An engagement can be scoped by phase, with staffing changes agreed as the work evolves and capacity is available.

Do you provide project management as well as technical roles?

Potential project roles include project managers, technical specialists, analysts, testers, documentation, and training support.

Is this a fixed-price project?

Support can be discussed as role-based staffing or a defined work package. Pricing structure and responsibilities must be agreed in the engagement documents.

How do you handle handoff?

Transition documentation, knowledge transfer, and support requirements can be included in the project scope.

Ready to discuss project-based support?

Share the role, scope, location, and start requirements. We'll review the request and follow up.
