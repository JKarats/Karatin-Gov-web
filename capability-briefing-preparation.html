How to Prepare for a Capability Briefing

Structure a focused discussion around mission needs, capability, proof, and next steps.

Karatin Government Solutions5 min read
Article overview ← Back to Resources Business & Teaming 5 min read

Structure a focused discussion around mission needs, capability, proof, and next steps.

Research the audience

Understand the agency, office, program, contracts, and likely requirements.

Lead with relevance

Connect capabilities to the buyer's mission and current priorities.

Use proof

Present experience, outcomes, methods, partners, and credible delivery plans.

Keep it concise

Avoid reading slides or listing every possible service.

End with next steps

Ask about priorities, acquisition paths, market research, and future engagement.
