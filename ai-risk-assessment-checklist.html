Evaluate data, security, privacy, legal, operational, and human-impact risks.

Data

Identify source data, sensitivity, quality, permissions, and retention.

Security

Review identity, access, integrations, logging, abuse, and incident response.

Privacy and legal

Assess personal data, notices, consent, intellectual property, and regulatory obligations.

Operational impact

Consider reliability, human review, change management, and business continuity.

Governance

Assign ownership, approval, monitoring, exceptions, and periodic reassessment.
