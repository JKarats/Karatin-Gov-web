Frequently asked questions

Can you staff both remote and on-site roles?

Tell us your location and work model. Remote, hybrid, and on-site requirements can be considered; candidate availability and client restrictions determine fit.

Do candidates have specific certifications?

We can include required certifications in the role criteria. Credentials and current status should be verified for individual candidates; none are assumed.

How quickly can you send candidates?

Shortlist timing depends on role, location, screening, and availability. We will discuss a realistic target after reviewing the request.

Can we request more than one position?

Yes. Share the number of roles, priorities, and approximate timing so the engagement can be scoped appropriately.

Ready to discuss it staffing?

Share the role, scope, location, and start requirements. We'll review the request and follow up.
